## Assignments
### [Embedded Lab](/year-3-semester-1/instrumentation-and-measurement/embedded-lab-report.pdf)
This experiment applied Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL) and Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL) to analyse a DC resistive circuit by calculating and measuring currents and voltages at different points. Additionally, the Wheatstone bridge was used to measure and verify resistance values, with both analogue and digital readings recorded and plotted to illustrate the relationship between resistance and voltage.
[Video Link](https://youtu.be/rwaZ7G1PT8E)

### [Assignment](/year-3-semester-1/instrumentation-and-measurement/assignment.pdf)
This assignment applies key concepts in measurement and instrumentation by solving a series of engineering problems involving measurement errors, uncertainty analysis, electrical circuit calculations, sensors, transducers and electronic instruments.
