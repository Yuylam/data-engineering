# Human Computer Interaction
This course introduces human-computer interaction theories and design processes, with its emphasis in applied user experience (UX) design.

- [Course content](#course-content)
- [Assignments](#assignments)
- [Reflection](#reflection)

## Course content
- Human Behaviour: Cognition aspects, emotional and social interactions
- Design: Interaction Design and Establishing requirements
- Implementation: Design, Prototype and Contructions
- Evaluation

## Reflection
It is always important to ensure the product that we create is intuitive and easy to use. To achieve this, we have to understand user behaviour. Besides, we should always conduct user testing to obtain feedback on our product to ensure the product is useful and can solve actual problems.
