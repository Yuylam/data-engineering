## 🧩 Tasks
### [Task 1 Self-Introduction](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rYb5O9OPwHVEPs4Z1r6IBf8Aewc1OLw2/view?usp=sharing)
This task required us to introduce ourselves and showcase our best photos. I realised that most of my photos focus on landscapes and capture memories from my travels.

As we went through everyone’s self-introduction, I was able to observe the different styles and focuses in photography among my classmates. Some focused on food, others on people or animals, while some presented a wide variety of subjects. This exposure helped me better understand different creative approaches and inspired me to explore beyond my usual landscape photography.

### [Task 2 Composition](/photocreative-services/photobook-composition.pdf)
In this task, we used DSLR cameras to capture photos around the university while applying composition techniques.

Before the photoshoot, I did some personal research to better prepare myself. I learned how to manually adjust camera settings such as aperture, ISO, and shutter speed. I also explored techniques like the Sunny 16 rule, which helped me better understand lighting and exposure when shooting outdoors.

### Task 3 HTAR Visit
During this task, we worked in groups to complete several components, including creating a visual art frame and constructing a rubbish bin using metal wire nets. We also produced a photobook and a video based on our experience.

My main responsibility was creating the photobook, so I focused primarily on capturing photos. This allowed me to apply the techniques I learned and document the process creatively. Working in a group also helped improve my teamwork and coordination skills, as each member contributed to different parts of the project.

## 🧠 Reflection
This course is very interesting and meaningful. As someone who enjoys photography, it provided a valuable opportunity to learn proper techniques. Instead of relying on automatic modes on phones or cameras, we learned how to manually adjust camera settings.

Additionally, we experienced photography in different environments, such as studio and outdoor settings, which helped broaden our skills and understanding.
