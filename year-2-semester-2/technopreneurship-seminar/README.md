# Technopreneurship Seminar
The course involves organising and handling events, such as a seminar, showcases and marketing. Students will form groups and choose one of three sections to work on: inviting panels, organising a showcase with relevant funding agencies, and inviting faculties and universities for marketing purposes. 

In this course, my group was involved in organising the seminar, particularly in emcee arrangement. I was particularly honoured to be the emcee of the event.
