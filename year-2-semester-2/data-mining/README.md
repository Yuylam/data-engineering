## Activities
### [Activity 1 Data Mining](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/activity1-data-mining.pdf)
This activity requires us to explore about data mining, including its role in database technology evolution, importance, architecture, steps, techniques, issues and challenges. It provides us with a basic theoretical idea of data mining.

### [Activity 2 Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD)](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/activity2-knowledge-discovery.pdf)
This activity required us to explore about knowledge discovery in databases, including its process and its relationship with machine learning.

### [Activity 3 Classification](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/activity3-classification.pdf)
This activity required us to explore about classification, including its importance, algorithm, model building process, evaluation metrics, challenges, cross-validation and ensemble methods. This activity provides us with a basic theoretical understanding of classification.

### [Activity 4 Classification Algorithm - XGBoost & Genetic Algorithm](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/activity4-xgboost-genetic-algorithm.pdf)
In this activity, the conceptual working, advantages, disadvantages and examples of XGBoost and Genetic Algorithm were studied. This activity provides us with a simple understanding of these two algorithms.

### [Activity 5 Association - FP-Growth Algorithm](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/activity5-fp-growth-algorithm.pdf)
In this activity, the FP-Growth Algorithm was studied. Including its working concept, advantages and disadvantages, and its application.

## Project
### [Assignment 1 Business Insights and Data Understanding](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/assignment1-business-insights-and-data-understanding.pdf)
In this assignment, five potential business insights that can be obtained from the "Spotify Tracks Dataset" were identified.

### [Project 1 Classification](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/project1-classification.pdf)
In this project, three classification models, including decision tree, SVM and random forest, were implemented to classify the data. The models were then evaluated based on its Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-score. As a result random forest performed the best compared to other models.

### [Project 2 Clustering](/year-2-semester-2/data-mining/project2-clustering.pdf)
In this project, two clustering models, including K-Means and GMM, were implemented to cluster the data, The models were then evaluated based on its Silhouette Score, Davies-Bouldin Index (DBI), Dunn Index and Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS). The evaluation concluded that K-Means algorithm consistently outperformed the GMM algorithm. 

## Reflection
Throughout the course, various machine learning was learned and implemented. It is very interesting to learn how the underlying pattern of the data could be identified. Although the machine learning models would not be always correct, as there are always other factors influencing the data, it was intriguing to explore different algorithms that were developed to compute and give meaning to the data.
