# Entrepreneurship and Innovation
This course introduces the fundamental concepts of entrepreneurship and entrepreneur. Besides, students are to conduct entrepreneurial or business activities by identifying, assembling and collaboratively managing the necessary resources.

- [Course content](#course-content)
- [Industry Visits and Talk](#industry-visits-and-talk)

## Course content
* Fundamental Concepts and Principles of Entrepreneurship
* Entrepreneurial Creativity and Innovation
* Developing Business Ideas Using Business Model Canvas (BMC)
* Business Idea Pitching and Persuasive Entrepreneurial Communication
* Marketing Strategies for Different Business Models
* Establishment of Business Start-ups
* Organisation Management for Business Start-ups
* Competitor Analysis and Strategies
* Marketing Planning for Business Start-up
* Production and Operations Planning for Business Start-Ups
* Financial Planning for Business Start-ups
